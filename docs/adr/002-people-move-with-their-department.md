# ADR-002 Waves are built by department, not alphabetically
Status: accepted

Context: Moving half of a team splits shared mailboxes, Teams and app roles across tenants.
Decision: Waves keep departments intact when they fit under the wave cap; lowest-risk departments go first; regulated desks go last after the runbook is proven.
Consequences: Wave sizes are uneven. The planner caps wave size and splits only departments larger than the cap.
